# Great Ideas - Kaminey (Kabo)

Valuable concepts, design directions, and feature roadmap ideas for Kaminey.

## Gameplay & Dynamics
1. **Innocence Trials & Duo Missions**: Duo side-missions that force two players to complete a collaborative task, either confirming innocence or giving a traitor conversational cover.
2. **Custom Palace Roles & Modifiers**:
   - *Khufiya (Secret Agent)*: Can silently verify if a specific player was visited at night.
   - *Senapati (Palace Guard)*: Can protect one player each night from elimination.
   - *Daayan (Witch/Alchemist)*: Holds a one-time potion of resurrection and a one-time poison vial.
3. **Haveli Chronicle (Post-Game Proclamation)**: Animated royal newspaper or scroll displaying a timeline recap: who killed whom, who voted for whom, biggest bluffs, and MVP awards.
4. **Mid-Game Host Pause & Rollback**: Safe host controls to pause timers or undo an accidental click without resetting the game.

## Immersion & Aesthetics
1. **Living Room TV Ambiance Mode**: Continuous ambient Haveli soundscapes (crickets/wind at night, royal sitar/tabla drone in the daytime lobby).
2. **Tactile Haptic Role Reveal**: Wax seal press-and-hold interaction with progressive vibration feedback.
3. **Turn-around Accusation Podium**: Highlighted spotlight on TV screen showing the accused player's avatar in dramatic candlelight.
