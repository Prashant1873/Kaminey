# Plan 09-01: Mobile Thumb Ergonomics & Progressive Disclosure Audit

## Objective
Enforce Fitts's Law (minimum 44x44px touch targets, bottom thumb reachability) and Hick's Law (progressive disclosure to minimize decision fatigue) across player screens.

## Scope
1. Update `PlayerVoting.jsx`:
   - Bottom-anchored thumb drawer for vote selection and confirmation.
   - Enforce 48px minimum touch height on suspect cards.
2. Update `PlayerDares.jsx`:
   - Progressive disclosure for mission objectives and ready checks.
   - Large tactile confirmation buttons within easy one-handed reach.
3. Update `PlayerNight.jsx`:
   - Night target selection card sizing and thumb zone optimization.

## Success Criteria
- All interactive controls on mobile measure at least 44x44px.
- Key decision triggers sit within natural thumb reach.
- Build compiles cleanly.
